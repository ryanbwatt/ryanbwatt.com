**Notes on Transformation.**
I spent a few weeks deciding what kind of business I want to build. The
answer was transformation: go into a small business, connect its tools into
one working layer, teach the owner to run it, and refresh how the business
presents itself. No product on day one; the first job is to prove the work
adds value.

Five companies shaped the idea.
<img src="/logos/longlake.svg" width="16" height="16" alt="" /> [Long Lake](https://llmh.com/)
runs the services businesses it buys on one shared AI platform, Nexus.
<img src="/logos/percepta.png" width="16" height="16" alt="" /> [Percepta](https://www.percepta.ai/)
embeds engineers inside large companies, backed by its own platform.
<img src="/logos/titan.png" width="16" height="16" alt="" /> [Titan](https://titanmsp.ai/)
buys IT service providers and rebuilds them around AI.
<img src="/logos/crescendo.png" width="16" height="16" alt="" /> [Crescendo](https://www.crescendo.ai/)
sells customer service as software plus the team that runs it, live in 30 days.
<img src="/logos/ap.png" width="16" height="16" alt="" /> [Applied Poetics](https://ap.ltd/)
is one small team for technology, image and design. All but the last work at
enterprise or acquisition scale. The small-business version is still open.

The model: start with galleries, architects and design studios. Sell one
fixed-scope engagement (a command center, owner training, a brand refresh),
take the first clients at little or no fee in exchange for publishing the
results, and turn what repeats into a named program with a retainer.
Reputation first, then network, then income.

To be the answer when owners ask an AI assistant for help: own a narrow
question, give the layer its own name, publish an explainer and case studies
with real numbers, get cited elsewhere, and ask the assistants a client's
questions every month to see who they name.

The first proof is [Gallery Assistant](https://gallery-assistant.pages.dev/),
a command center for an art gallery that pairs each work with the collectors
most likely to want it and drafts outreach for review.
