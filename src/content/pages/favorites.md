Things I've read or watched and kept.

- **[Third places are disappearing](https://theweek.com/culture-life/third-places-disappearing)**
  — On the slow loss of the third place: the cafés, bars, and public rooms
  that are neither home nor work, and what goes with them.

- **[Bowling Alone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowling_Alone)** — Robert
  Putnam's account of the collapse of American civic and social participation,
  and the idea of social capital that came out of it.

- **[A Psychologically Rich Life: Beyond Happiness and Meaning](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/releases/rev-rev0000317.pdf)**
  — Oishi and Westgate argue there's a third dimension to a good life
  alongside happiness and meaning: psychological richness, the kind that comes
  from varied experience that changes how you see things.

- **[Cameras Complicated the Truth in Images. A.I. Will Kill It.](https://www.nytimes.com/2025/06/10/opinion/photography-truth-ai-images.html)**
  — Photography's claim to be evidence was always shakier than we treated it,
  and generative models finish the job.

- **[Love+War](https://films.nationalgeographic.com/love-war)** — Chai
  Vasarhelyi and Jimmy Chin follow the conflict photographer Lynsey Addario,
  and the pull between the work of documenting war and the rest of a life.

- **[Blink](https://films.nationalgeographic.com/blink)** — A Montreal family
  takes their children around the world after three of the four are diagnosed
  with retinitis pigmentosa, to give them things worth remembering while they
  can still see.

- **[The unsettling message for investors from the financial cycle](https://www.ft.com/content/f48ffe98-acdf-4bb9-8c16-8037715c022e)**
  — The FT on where the long financial cycle sits and what that implies for
  anyone invested through it.

- **[How exercise influences the brain](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7381101/)**
  — A review of the mechanisms by which physical activity changes the
  structure and function of the brain.

- **[Diet quality and the precursors of early-onset colorectal cancer](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33136160)**
  — On how overall diet quality relates to the lesions that precede colorectal
  cancer in people under fifty.

- **[Large study links gum disease with dementia](https://www.nia.nih.gov/news/large-study-links-gum-disease-dementia)**
  — Periodontal disease turns up as an association with later dementia risk,
  which is a stranger result than it first sounds.
